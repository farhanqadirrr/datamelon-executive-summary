# Executive Summary Dashboard

## Project overview

Portfolio documentation for an executive-summary dashboard completed during the DataMelon Labs Industry Project. The dashboard brings rolling 12-month business performance into one Power BI view, combining sales, transactions, customer activity, loyalty participation, and commercial breakdowns.

## Business problem

Business stakeholders need a concise view of overall performance and the factors behind it. This dashboard is designed to help them monitor sales and customer activity over time, compare regional and store-format contribution, and identify where loyalty participation and department/category performance are strongest.

## Tools and technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- Dashboard reporting and business-performance analysis

## Dashboard scope

The dashboard includes:

- Rolling 12-month KPIs for sales, transactions, customers, spend per customer, and loyalty measures
- Monthly trend comparison for overall sales and loyalty sales
- Regional sales contribution and a city-level sales table
- Store-format and department performance, including loyalty participation
- Loyalty-tier, payment-method, and age-group distributions
- Category-level sales, customer penetration, transaction, and average-transaction-value measures

## Key insights from the dashboard

- The reported rolling 12-month period shows **₹895.3M in sales**, **1.1M transactions**, and **14,345 customers who shopped**.
- Compared with the preceding three-month period, the latest three months show **36.8% sales growth**, **35.8% transaction growth**, and **45.2% loyalty-sales growth**.
- **North** is the largest regional contributor, with **₹238.0M** in sales and **26.58%** of reported sales contribution; **Central** is the smallest of the five regions shown, at **₹124.7M**.
- **Grocery & Staples** is the largest department by sales at **₹298.3M**, followed by **Beverages** at **₹179.5M**.
- Loyalty sales total **₹497.3M** and account for **55.6%** of reported sales. Within the loyalty programme, the **Silver** tier contributes the largest share of loyalty sales (**74.8%**).
- The dashboard identifies a set of categories that together drive 80% of overall business sales, supporting focused category monitoring.
